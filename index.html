Launch to scale

Product & GTMCrossian2026

Challenge

Take a new product from zero to profitable scale, and decide quickly which creatives and offers deserve budget before committing to a minimum order (MOQ).

Insight

Buyers decide in the first seconds of an ad: a clear, visual proof of the product's main benefit converts better than brand claims, so the USP has to be shown, not told.

Strategy

  • Built positioning, USPs and GTM messaging from consumer insight and competitor analysis (FAB & DPA).
  • Scripted and generated AI video ads (Higgsfield) to test many angles fast and cheaply.
  • Ran pre-MOQ tests on CTR, CPM, CVR and ROAS, then made scale / kill / iterate calls.

Performance & P&LCrossian2026

Challenge

Keep the product profitable as spend grows, with Merchandising, Fulfillment, Creative and Ads all moving at different speeds.

Strategy

  • Weekly and monthly performance reviews that turn results into a profit plan: pricing and upsell-by-quantity offers, product mix, promotions and forecast.
  • Analysed colour and size distribution to direct page and ad creative optimisation.
  • One launch timeline and content plan shared by every team.

Result

Grew product sales from $47K a month in August to $119.4K in November (+154%), while marketing spend rose from $30.2K to $56.4K (+87%). ROAS from 1.5 to 2.4.

Slimcase Rex

Product launchiPhone 15 & 16 Pro MaxSep–Nov 2025

Challenge

Launch Rex, Slimcase's Kevlar case for iPhone 15 Pro Max and 16 Pro Max, and make a premium, technical product feel desirable, not just tough.

Insight

People who buy a rugged case don't want to look rugged. They want protection that disappears into a slim, premium feel, and an identity to match.

Strategy

  • Developed the product concept (visual and message) across markets and platforms: website, Meta and e-commerce; led the team's content and creative production.
  • Positioned Rex as “Crafted for the Champion”: strength as a mindset.
  • Four proof points: original Kevlar (5× stronger than steel), Slimpact™ 2.0 with an aluminium frame (3 m drop), a Dark Metallic finish and a slim pocket fit.
  • Rolled out over three months, from the launch key visual to outdoor and everyday content.

Result

Launch post: 331 reactions, 116 comments and 8 shares.
